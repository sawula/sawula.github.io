03 — Spec Cards

A design approach

A framework for designing learning experiences through structured specification. What they are, how to make them, and why they help.

Spec cards are a way of thinking carefully about what a learning experience is actually supposed to do — before you build it. This booklet walks through the approach, with examples and code.
