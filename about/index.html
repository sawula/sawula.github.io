Poolish.org is a personal site for sharing the things I'm working on: recipes from the kitchen, notebooks from a data science course I've been building, and a framework for designing learning experiences called spec cards.

The name comes from the bread starter — a poolish is a simple mixture of flour, water, and a little yeast that ferments overnight and gives the final loaf its depth and flavor. It takes patience. Most good things do.
